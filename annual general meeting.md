## Annual General Meeting & Anniversary Celebration

## Overview

The **Annual General Meeting (AGM) & Anniversary Celebration 2026** marked a defining institutional milestone for the **Global Surgical Students Network (GSSN)**, commemorating the successful completion of its inaugural operational year following the organization’s 2025 pilot phase.

Held on **January 17, 2026**, the gathering served as both a formal organizational review and a large-scale community celebration, bringing together approximately **130 participants**, including executive members, council representatives, departmental leads, and general members from across the network.

The AGM was designed to reinforce organizational transparency, academic collaboration, interdepartmental accountability, and long-term community engagement while simultaneously celebrating the collective growth and achievements of the organization’s foundational year.

---

## Institutional Context

## Organizational Significance

As GSSN transitioned from its pilot-stage developmental structure into a more established organizational framework, the AGM represented:

- The first comprehensive annual institutional review
- A platform for departmental accountability and reporting
- A milestone celebration of community growth
- A strategic checkpoint for future organizational expansion
- A reflection of evolving student-led surgical collaboration initiatives

The event highlighted the maturation of GSSN from an emerging student initiative into a structured, academically active, and community-oriented network.

---

## Event Structure

The AGM was divided into two major segments:

| Segment | Primary Focus |
|---|---|
| Formal Institutional Session | Organizational review, reporting, academic presentations, transparency |
| Community Celebration Session | Anniversary celebration, engagement activities, member interaction |

---

## Formal Session

## Organizational Review & Reporting

The formal session focused on evaluating the organization’s progress throughout its inaugural year.

### Key Components Included

- Comprehensive yearly organizational review
- Annual departmental and wing reports
- Executive reflections on institutional development
- Operational transparency discussions
- Strategic insights for future planning

Each departmental lead presented updates regarding:

- Activities conducted
- Internal progress
- Challenges encountered
- Collaborative outcomes
- Planned future directions

This reporting structure encouraged institutional accountability while also documenting the collective contributions of multiple working divisions within GSSN.

---

## Academic Presentations

The AGM additionally incorporated a series of **mid-academic presentations**, initially intended for presentation at **ASiT**, thereby integrating educational value into the organizational gathering.

### Academic Objectives

- Encourage academic participation among members
- Promote presentation and communication skills
- Foster collaborative scholarly discussion
- Maintain GSSN’s educational identity alongside community engagement

The inclusion of academic sessions reinforced the organization’s emphasis on balancing professional development with community-building initiatives.

---

## Interactive Q&A Session

An open interactive discussion and Q&A session was conducted to encourage:

- Organizational transparency
- Member participation
- Constructive dialogue
- Leadership accessibility
- Community feedback integration

This segment created an environment where members could directly engage with leadership regarding organizational direction, operational concerns, and future initiatives.

## Anniversary Celebration Segment

Following the formal proceedings, the event transitioned into a celebratory and community-focused atmosphere.

## Celebration Activities

### Anniversary Cake-Cutting Ceremony

A ceremonial cake-cutting session was conducted to commemorate GSSN’s first institutional anniversary and symbolize the completion of its foundational year.

---

### Community Reflection Video

A featured presentation titled:

> **“What GSSN Means to Our Council Members”**

showcased personal reflections, experiences, and testimonials from council members regarding their journey within the organization.

The video emphasized:

- Community identity
- Shared growth experiences
- Interpersonal collaboration
- Organizational culture
- Emotional connection to the network

---

### Team Games & Interactive Engagement

Multiple interactive activities were organized to strengthen interpersonal bonding and encourage member participation.

Activities included:

- Team-based games
- Informal collaborative challenges
- Community interaction sessions
- Recognition-based engagement activities

These segments promoted inclusivity and strengthened cross-departmental interaction among members.

---

### Member Recognition Titles

Special appreciation and recognition titles were awarded to members for their contributions, participation, leadership, and engagement throughout the year.

This recognition segment aimed to:

- Encourage continued involvement
- Acknowledge member contributions
- Reinforce positive organizational culture
- Promote morale and motivation

### Surgical Trivia Activities

Educational surgical trivia sessions were incorporated as a blend of:
