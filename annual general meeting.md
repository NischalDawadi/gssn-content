## Annual General Meeting & Anniversary Celebration

### Institutional Overview

The **Annual General Meeting (AGM) & Anniversary Celebration 2026** marked a defining institutional milestone for the **Global Surgical Students Network (GSSN)**, commemorating the successful completion of its inaugural operational year following the 2025 pilot phase.

Held on **January 17, 2026**, the event brought together approximately **130 participants**, including executive members, council representatives, departmental leads, and general members across the network.

The programme functioned as both a formal organizational review and a community-wide celebration, reinforcing transparency, academic collaboration, and long-term institutional development.

---

## Institutional Context

As GSSN transitioned from its pilot structure into a more established framework, the AGM represented:

- The first comprehensive annual institutional review  
- A structured platform for departmental accountability  
- A milestone celebration of community consolidation  
- A strategic checkpoint for organizational expansion  
- A reflection of evolving student-led surgical collaboration  

This transition highlighted the maturation of GSSN into a structured, academically active, and community-oriented network.

---

## Event Structure

The programme was organized into two core segments:

- Formal Institutional Session: organizational review, reporting, academic presentations, and interactive discussion  
- Community Celebration Session: anniversary observance, engagement activities, and member interaction  

This dual structure ensured a balance between academic accountability and community cohesion.

---

## Formal Institutional Session

### Organizational Review and Reporting

The formal session focused on evaluating progress across the organization’s inaugural year.

Key components included:

- Annual organizational review  
- Departmental and wing reports  
- Executive reflections on institutional development  
- Operational transparency discussions  
- Strategic direction planning  

Each departmental lead presented structured updates covering:

- Activities conducted  
- Internal progress  
- Challenges encountered  
- Collaborative outcomes  
- Future planning directions  

This framework strengthened institutional accountability while documenting multi-departmental contributions.

---

### Academic Presentations

The AGM included mid-academic presentations originally prepared for **ASiT**, integrating academic value into the institutional setting.

Objectives included:

- Encouraging academic participation among members  
- Enhancing presentation and communication skills  
- Promoting scholarly discussion  
- Maintaining educational engagement alongside organizational development  

This reinforced GSSN’s dual focus on academic growth and community building.

---

### Interactive Q&A Session

An open discussion session enabled direct engagement between members and leadership.

Key focus areas:

- Organizational transparency  
- Member feedback and participation  
- Operational clarity  
- Future strategic direction  
- Leadership accessibility  

This segment strengthened internal communication and participatory governance.

---

## Community Celebration Session

### Anniversary Commemoration

The celebration segment marked GSSN’s first institutional anniversary through a formal cake-cutting ceremony, symbolizing the completion of its foundational year.

---

### Community Reflection Presentation

A featured video titled **“What GSSN Means to Our Council Members”** presented reflections from council members on their experiences within the organization.

Themes included:

- Organizational identity  
- Shared growth experiences  
- Collaboration and teamwork  
- Institutional culture  
- Community belonging  

---

### Engagement Activities

A series of interactive activities were conducted to strengthen member engagement:

- Team-based collaborative games  
- Informal group interactions  
- Community bonding sessions  
- Recognition-based activities  

These initiatives enhanced cross-departmental interaction and inclusivity.

---

### Member Recognition

Members were acknowledged for their contributions and engagement throughout the year.

Recognition aimed to:

- Encourage continued participation  
- Acknowledge individual contributions  
- Reinforce organizational culture  
- Support leadership development  

---

### Surgical Trivia Session

A structured trivia segment introduced educational engagement within the celebration context.

It combined:

- Academic reinforcement  
- Interactive learning  
- Community participation  
- Informal knowledge exchange
