## Annual General Meeting & Anniversary Celebration 

Global Surgical Students Network (GSSN) marked a major milestone with its first **Annual General Meeting (AGM)**, held on **January 17, 2026**, commemorating its inaugural year following the 2025 pilot phase.

With an estimated 130 participants, AGM brought together executives, council members, and general members for a comprehensive reflection on GSSN’s journey.

---

## Event Highlights

The formal session featured:

- A yearly organizational review
- Annual reports from individual wing leads
- Mid-academic presentations originally planned for ASiT
- An interactive Q&A session encouraging transparency and engagement

The event later transitioned into a more celebratory segment, including:

- Anniversary cake-cutting ceremony
- Community reflection video:
  **“What GSSN Means to Our Council Members”**
- Team games and member recognition titles
- Surgical trivia activities blending education with engagement

---

## Objectives

- Reflect on GSSN’s progress and achievements
- Strengthen member engagement and organizational transparency
- Celebrate the completion of GSSN’s foundational year
- Encourage collaboration and future participation

---

## Key Outcomes

- Successfully gathered approximately 130 participants
- Strengthened organizational unity and culture
- Showcased academic and departmental achievements
- Reinforced member participation and leadership interaction

---

## Community Impact

The AGM concluded on a high note, capturing both the achievements of GSSN’s founding year and the strong sense of community driving its future forward.

The event established a foundation for future annual gatherings centered around transparency, celebration, academic collaboration, and organizational growth.

---

## Event Information

- **Date:** January 17, 2026
- **Organized By:** Global Surgical Students Network (GSSN)
- **Format:** Hybrid Community & Academic Gathering
- **Participants:** ~130 attendees
