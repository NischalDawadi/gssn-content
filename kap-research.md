## Mastering KAP Survey Research: Design, Analysis & Publication

### Research Capacity Building Session for Undergraduate Medical Students

> A structured research-focused academic webinar conducted by the GSSN Research Wing, aimed at strengthening foundational and applied research competencies in Knowledge, Attitude, and Practice (KAP) survey methodology.

---

## Programme Overview

As part of its continued commitment to developing research capacity among medical students, the **Global Surgical Students Network (GSSN)** conducted a structured academic session in 2026 titled:

**“Mastering KAP Survey Research: Likert Scale Design, Analysis, and Publication for Undergraduate Medical Students.”**

The session was designed to bridge the gap between theoretical understanding and publication-oriented research practice, with a strong emphasis on applied methodological skills.

---

## Core Focus Areas

The webinar concentrated on essential components of KAP-based research design and execution:

- Design and structuring of KAP surveys  
- Development of Likert scale-based instruments  
- Statistical analysis and interpretation of survey data  
- Identification of common methodological errors in undergraduate research  
- Principles of publication-oriented research writing  

---

## Methodological Training Objectives

The session aimed to strengthen participant capability in:

- Constructing valid and reliable research instruments  
- Applying structured analytical approaches to survey data  
- Translating raw data into meaningful research findings  
- Understanding the pathway from research design to publication  

> The emphasis was placed on developing both conceptual clarity and practical research execution skills.

---

## Academic Leadership

The session was conducted under the guidance of **Lilasha Poudel**, a distinguished researcher and academic contributor with expertise in research methodology and editorial processes.

Her professional experience includes:

- Protocol development and editorial work at *BMC Trials*  
- Editorial contributions at *JNMA*  
- Mentorship in research methodology and statistical analysis for early-career researchers  

Her contribution provided participants with a structured understanding of real-world research and publication standards.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Participants gained structured insight into:

- End-to-end design of KAP survey studies  
- Construction of Likert scale-based research tools  
- Data analysis frameworks for survey research  
- Recognition of common methodological pitfalls  
- Application of research principles in evidence-based medical practice  

---

## Session Delivery

The webinar was conducted in an interactive format, ensuring active engagement between participants and the facilitator.

Key characteristics of delivery included:

- Conceptual clarity through structured explanation  
- Practical examples of survey design and analysis  
- Interactive discussion and query resolution  
- Focus on publication readiness and research applicability  

---

## Impact

The session contributed to strengthening GSSN’s broader research ecosystem by:

- Enhancing undergraduate research literacy  
- Promoting structured methodological thinking  
- Encouraging publication-oriented academic output  
- Supporting early-career research development  
- Reinforcing evidence-based medical education principles
