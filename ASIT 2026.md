## ASiT 2026 Achievements

### International Academic Participation and Research Output

> A significant academic milestone for the Global Surgical Students Network (GSSN), marked by strong participation and research presentations at ASiT 2026, demonstrating growing scholarly engagement in surgical education and global surgery.

---

## Overview

GSSN actively participated in **ASiT 2026**, providing a structured academic platform for council members and contributors to present research work in surgical education and related disciplines.

This participation represents a key milestone in the organization’s progression toward sustained international academic engagement and research dissemination.

---

## Academic Output

The 2026 cycle recorded a total of **21 research acceptances**, reflecting strong scholarly productivity within the GSSN community.

### Breakdown of Acceptances

- 17 Poster Presentations  
- 4 Oral Presentations  

---

## Institutional Significance

This achievement reflects the increasing academic maturity of the GSSN research ecosystem and highlights the sustained commitment of its members toward producing and presenting scholarly work on international platforms.

Key institutional dimensions include:

- Expansion of student-led research output  
- Strengthening of academic writing and presentation capacity  
- Increased visibility in international surgical forums  
- Consolidation of a research-driven organizational culture  

---

## Contributing Framework

The success at ASiT 2026 was enabled through a coordinated multi-layer contribution system involving:

- Presenting authors and research teams  
- GSSN academic contributors and reviewers  
- Organizational support from multiple GSSN wings  
- Strategic academic guidance from leadership  

This collaborative structure reinforced a sustainable model for academic production and dissemination.

---

## Leadership Recognition

Special recognition is extended to **Dr. Bishow Karki**, whose continued academic leadership and guidance have been instrumental in shaping GSSN’s research direction and strengthening its international academic presence.

---

## Impact

The ASiT 2026 participation underscores several long-term impacts:

- Strengthened global research visibility  
- Increased acceptance of student-led surgical research  
- Enhanced academic confidence among members  
- Reinforced institutional commitment to evidence-based practice  
- Expansion of collaborative research culture within GSSN
