# AI in Surgery: Basic Foundations and Clinical Applications

## Educational Webinar on Artificial Intelligence and Modern Surgical Practice

## 1. Executive Summary

The **GSSN Education Wing** conducted **“AI in Surgery: Basic Foundations and Clinical Applications”** on **21 April 2026**, providing participants with an introduction to the role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in contemporary surgical practice, education, simulation, and healthcare.

The session was delivered by **Dr. Suryodaya Bikram Shahi**, a graduate researcher in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision with a Master's degree in Data Science and a Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering.

The webinar explored the evolution of AI systems and their increasing relevance to medicine and surgery. Topics included multimodal AI, surgical simulation, assistive technologies, real-time decision-making, surgical navigation, robotic surgery, telesurgery, and AI-supported clinical decision-making.

Particular emphasis was placed on understanding AI as a tool that can complement surgical expertise rather than replace surgeons. The session also addressed challenges related to reliability, accountability, liability, and the complexity of applying AI in dynamic clinical environments.

A total of **75 participants** attended the programme.

---

## 2. Background and Rationale

Artificial Intelligence is increasingly being incorporated into healthcare, including areas relevant to surgical education, diagnosis, imaging, navigation, simulation, robotics, and clinical decision support.

As medical education evolves alongside these technologies, future clinicians increasingly require an understanding of how AI systems function, where they may be applied, and what limitations and challenges accompany their use.

The programme was designed to introduce medical students and young doctors to the foundations and emerging applications of AI in surgery while encouraging critical discussion about the role of AI in future surgical practice.

---

## 3. Aim and Objectives

### Aim

To introduce participants to the fundamental concepts and emerging clinical applications of **Artificial Intelligence in surgery** and to develop awareness of its potential role in modern surgical practice and education.

### Specific Objectives

The session aimed to:

- Introduce the fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence.
- Explain the evolving role of AI in modern surgery.
- Introduce multimodal AI systems and contemporary AI approaches.
- Explore applications of AI in surgical simulation and training.
- Discuss AI-assisted clinical decision-making and surgical technologies.
- Examine emerging applications including robotics, telesurgery, imaging, and navigation.
- Discuss potential challenges related to reliability, accountability, and liability.
- Encourage participants to consider the implications of AI for future medical and surgical practice.

- Concept-based teaching
- Introduction to contemporary AI technologies
- Discussion of surgical applications
- Examples of emerging technologies
- Exploration of future possibilities in surgical education and care

---

## 9. Discussion and Significance

The programme provided participants with an interdisciplinary introduction to a rapidly developing area of healthcare technology.

A central theme of the session was that AI should be considered as a **companion to surgeons rather than a replacement for surgeons**. The discussion therefore extended beyond technological capabilities to consider how AI may support clinical decision-making, education, simulation, and patient care.

The range of applications discussed demonstrated the potential breadth of AI integration across the surgical pathway, from diagnostic assistance and imaging to intraoperative support, postoperative prediction, and surgical education.
