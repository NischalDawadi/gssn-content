# GSSN–IGI IT² NEXUS Healthcare Innovation Grand Challenge 2026

## Programme Overview

The **GSSN–IGI IT² NEXUS Healthcare Innovation Grand Challenge** is an innovation programme designed to empower medical students, junior doctors, and multidisciplinary innovators to develop **affordable, scalable, and sustainable healthcare solutions** through frugal innovation and surgical simulation.

### Programme Information

| Item | Details |

| **Theme** | Healthcare Innovation through Frugal Surgical Simulation |

| **Application Deadline** | **27 September 2026** |
| **Expert Evaluation** | **28 September – 1 October 2026** |

---

## 1. Grand Challenge

### **Grand Challenge Question**

> **How might we use frugal surgical simulation to develop affordable, scalable, and sustainable healthcare innovations that improve surgical education and healthcare delivery in resource-limited settings?**

Participants are encouraged to think beyond simulation devices alone and explore **educational, technological, engineering, digital, and systems-based innovations** that address real healthcare needs.

---

## 2. Aim

To support participants in developing innovative, practical, and affordable healthcare solutions through surgical simulation, with the potential to strengthen surgical education and healthcare delivery in resource-limited settings.

---

## 3. Who Can Participate?

**Both solo participants and teams are welcome and equally appreciated.**

Applications are open to:

- Medical students
- Foundation and intern doctors
- Early-career trainees
- Engineers
- Designers
- Technology professionals
- Other multidisciplinary innovators
- GSSN members

Participants may apply **individually or as part of a team**.

---

## 4. Innovation Theme

### Healthcare Innovation through Frugal Surgical Simulation

Participants are encouraged to develop solutions that are:

- Affordable
- Accessible
- Practical
- Sustainable
- Scalable

Possible areas include:

- Low-cost surgical simulators
- Portable simulation systems
- AI-assisted or virtual simulation
- Digital education platforms
- Skills assessment tools
- Curriculum innovation
- Mobile simulation
- Locally manufactured solutions
- Systems that improve access to surgical education

---

## 5. Application Requirements

Applicants should submit:

1. Completed application form
2. **One-page innovation proposal**
3. Short team/participant introduction or proposal video

The proposal should briefly address:

- What healthcare problem are you solving?
- Why is the problem important?
- What is your proposed innovation?
- How does surgical simulation contribute?
- How is the solution affordable and sustainable?
- What would the prototype or first version look like?
- What potential impact could it have?

## 8. Expert Evaluation

Shortlisted participants will have their innovations evaluated by an expert panel.

Evaluation will consider:

- Healthcare relevance
- Innovation potential
- Feasibility
- Frugal design
- Scalability
- Sustainability
- Potential impact
- Quality of presentation

---

## 9. Mentorship

Selected finalists will receive expert mentorship from **3–9 October 2026**.

Mentorship may include:

- Clinical guidance
- Surgical simulation expertise
- Technical and engineering advice
- Research guidance
- Prototype development
- Presentation and pitch refinement

Participants retain ownership and responsibility for their innovations.

---

## 10. Grand Finale

### **11 October 2026**

Finalists will present their innovations before the judging panel.

The finale will include:

- Innovation presentation
- Question-and-answer session
- Final evaluation
- Recognition and awards

Participants may demonstrate prototypes, proof-of-concepts, or supporting materials where appropriate.

---

## 11. Assessment Principles

Projects will be assessed based on their ability to address meaningful healthcare challenges through practical and sustainable innovation.

Key considerations include:

- **Healthcare Need**
- **Innovation**
- **Frugal Design**
- **Feasibility**
- **Impact**
- **Scalability**
- **Sustainability**
- **Presentation**

---

## 12. Participant Expectations

Participants are expected to:

- Submit original work.
- Meet programme deadlines.
- Participate actively in scheduled activities.
- Work collaboratively and professionally.
- Respect confidentiality and intellectual property.
- Consider patient safety and ethical implications.

---

## 13. Intellectual Property

Intellectual property developed by participants remains with the respective participant or team unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Participation does not transfer ownership of an innovation, prototype, design, software, or research output to GSSN, IGI, or programme partners.

---

## 14. Responsible Innovation

Participants should ensure that their innovations are:

- Clinically meaningful
- Evidence-informed
- Safe and responsible
- Affordable and accessible
- Appropriate for resource-limited settings
- Sustainable

Technology-based solutions should also consider privacy, data protection, bias, transparency, and appropriate human oversight.

---

## 15. Post-Challenge Opportunities

Selected high-potential projects may be considered for:

- Continued mentorship
- Prototype development
- Research collaboration
- Pilot project development
- Funding or bursary opportunities
- Conference presentations
- Publication opportunities

---

## 16. Closing Statement

The **GSSN–IGI IT² NEXUS Healthcare Innovation Grand Challenge 2026** aims to inspire the next generation of healthcare innovators to transform surgical education and healthcare delivery through **clinical insight, multidisciplinary collaboration, frugal design, and practical innovation**.

Whether participating **solo or as a team**, participants are invited to develop solutions that are **affordable, sustainable, scalable, and relevant to real-world healthcare needs**.
